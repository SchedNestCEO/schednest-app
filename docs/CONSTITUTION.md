# The SchedNest Constitution

Version 1.0

> A platform built to coordinate life with trust, intelligence, and human control.

## Mission

To help people and organizations coordinate the most important parts of their lives through one trusted adaptive intelligence.

## Vision

To become the world's most trusted adaptive coordination platform—one that learns, adapts, and grows with people throughout every stage of life while always keeping humans in control.

## The Five Pillars

1. **One Platform** — Student, Teams, Med, Business, Life, and future industry experiences share one foundation.
2. **One Birdy** — Birdy learns responsibly, explains itself, and never replaces human judgment.
3. **One Coordination Engine** — Assignments, appointments, projects, tasks, meetings, medications, requests, and workflows are all forms of coordination.
4. **Human Control** — Birdy recommends; people decide. Automation occurs only inside approved boundaries.
5. **Trust Above Everything** — Privacy, security, transparency, accessibility, and reliability are requirements.

## Platform Principles

- Platform before products.
- Reuse before rebuilding.
- Simplicity wins.
- Explain every recommendation.
- Learn continuously.
- Build for life.
- Security before convenience.
- Accessibility from day one.
- Human judgment always wins.
- Ship small and improve from evidence.

## AI Principles

Users control what Birdy remembers, forgets, accesses, and automates. Birdy communicates why, confidence, alternatives, consequences, and undo paths.

## Non-Negotiables

Birdy never diagnoses patients, prescribes medication, makes irreversible high-impact decisions without approval, hides its reasoning, manipulates users for attention, sells personal data, or behaves as a surveillance system.

Users can always override Birdy, inspect memory, edit or delete memory, export data, delete data, pause AI learning, revoke access, and review activity history.

## North Star

> Does this help people coordinate the important parts of their lives more effectively while preserving their trust and control?

## Motto

> Coordinate Life. Empower People.

## Engineering Motto

> One Platform. One Birdy. Infinite Possibilities.
