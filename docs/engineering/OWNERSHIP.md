# Subsystem Ownership

- Business owns business scheduling and customer workflows.
- Student owns academic planning and student workflows.
- Teams owns team coordination workflows.
- Medical owns medical scheduling workflows.
- Life owns personal scheduling workflows.
- Shared Platform owns cross-product event transport and explicitly shared services.
- Founder OS owns administrative visibility and controlled operations.
- Birdy owns AI interpretation, recommendations, and approved agent workflows.
- Performance Center will own performance history, testing metadata, and capacity analysis.
